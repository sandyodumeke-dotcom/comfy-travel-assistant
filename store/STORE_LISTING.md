**Full description**
Never leave the passport behind again. Comfy Travel Assistant reads your travel checklist aloud at the times you choose, in a lady's or a gentleman's voice, and repeats it until you press Stop.

REMINDERS THAT ACTUALLY SPEAK
- Type or say your items, then pick one or more reminder times
- She says: "Good morning Amaka, don't forget to take the passport, tickets and charger"
- Repeats with "I repeat" until you tap Stop. Works when the app is closed
- Mark must-have items with a star, snooze, and get a heads-up before the reminder

PLAN THE WHOLE TRIP
- Ready-made packing lists, readiness checklist and flight and hotel notes
- Weather forecast, world clock, currency converter, tip splitter, phrasebook
- Luggage weight checker and trip budget tracker
- Dark mode, four colour themes, smooth animations

PRIVATE BY DESIGN. Your lists stay on your phone. No account, no ads.

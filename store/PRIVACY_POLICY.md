# Privacy Policy for Comfy Travel Assistant

Last updated: [DATE]

Comfy Travel Assistant ("the app") helps you remember what to take on a trip by reading your reminders aloud.

**Data stored on your device.** The items, times, trip details, notes, budget, luggage weights and settings you enter are stored only on your phone. We do not run a server and we cannot see this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

**Data we do not collect.** The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect your name, contacts, files or precise location.

**Optional online tools.** If you use the weather tool, the city name you type is sent to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to get a forecast. If you tap "Get live rate" in the currency tool, the two currency codes you type are sent to ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com). These services receive your IP address as part of any internet request. Nothing else is sent.

**Permissions.** Notifications (to show your reminder), foreground service and wake lock (to read a reminder aloud while your screen is off), alarms (to start at the exact time you chose), run at startup (to restore your reminders after a restart) and vibration.

**Children.** The app is not directed to children under 13.
